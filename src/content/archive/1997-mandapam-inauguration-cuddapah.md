These photographs were taken on 22 May 1997, the day the Mandapam was officially inaugurated in
Cuddapah — a milestone already recorded in the [Heritage chronology](/heritage). The statue itself
predates this occasion; the photographs record the inauguration day's offerings and the community
gathered around it, not the statue's installation.
