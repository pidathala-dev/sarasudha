# Instagram launch — 2-week content calendar

A simple posting cadence for the six launch posts, spaced out rather than
dumped all at once, plus a few lightweight extras. Adjust dates to whenever
the account actually goes live — these are relative day numbers (Day 1 = launch day).

Post each feed image with its matching caption from `captions/launch-captions.md`
and the hashtag block from `captions/hashtags.md` as the first comment.

## Story activity (between feed posts, to keep the account active)

- **Day 2** — Repost Post 1 to Story (use `stories/01-...-story-1080x1920.png`)
  with a **question sticker**: "What does Sarasudha make you curious about?"
- **Day 6** — Repost Post 3 (Heritage) to Story with a **poll sticker**:
  "Do you have a family connection to Cuddapah's music scene? Yes / Tell us more"
  (drive answers to DMs — this is a soft, honest way to start surfacing real
  archive contributors, not a manufactured engagement bait).
- **Day 11** — Repost Post 6 (Participate) to Story with a **link sticker**
  to sarasudha.in/participate.

## Cross-post

- **Day 8** — Share Post 4 (One Tradition. Many Expressions.) to a LinkedIn
  post for the owner's/organisation's LinkedIn presence, if one exists —
  the "scope of music" message reads well to a more general/professional
  audience and doesn't depend on Instagram-specific formatting.

## After Day 13

Once the six launch posts are live, move to a slower, sustainable cadence
(e.g. one post every 4–7 days) built from real developments — a new artist
joining, an archive item recovered, an event confirmed — rather than
continuing to invent content. See `docs/CONTENT_GUIDE.md` in the main repo
for what's safe to publish about Sarasudha at any given time.
