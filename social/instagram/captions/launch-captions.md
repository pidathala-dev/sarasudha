# Launch captions

One caption per launch post, plus a shorter alternate for each and a first-comment
hashtag block (see `hashtags.md`). Tone: warm, specific, culturally rooted — never
"marketing voice." Nothing here claims anything the website doesn't already state;
see `docs/CONTENT_GUIDE.md` in the main repo before writing future captions.

---

## Post 1 — Introducing Sarasudha

**Caption:**

Sarasudha is a new space for music — Carnatic tradition, light music and
contemporary expression, held in one place instead of three separate boxes.

We're just getting started. No polished archive yet, no artist roster, no
events calendar — just an honest beginning and an open door.

Rooted in tradition. Open to every note.

More at sarasudha.in.

**Short version:**

A new space for music begins. Carnatic tradition, light music and
contemporary expression — together. Rooted in tradition. Open to every note.
→ sarasudha.in

## Post 2 — The Name

**Caption:**

Sarasudha comes from two names, not one word invented for a brand.

Sarala — simplicity, sincerity, grace.
Sudha — nectar, sweetness, richness.

Put together, they became a name for something bigger than either: a
platform where music can be both honest and rich at once.

Two names. One musical expression.

**Short version:**

Sarala (simplicity, sincerity, grace) + Sudha (nectar, sweetness, richness)
= Sarasudha. Two names. One musical expression.

## Post 3 — Rooted in 1984

**Caption:**

Before Sarasudha, there was Annamacharya Kalabharati — a cultural
initiative registered in Cuddapah in 1984 (Regn. No. 217/1984), bringing
music, artists and community together.

Sarasudha is a new chapter inspired by that cultural journey — not a claim
that it's the same organisation, but an honest acknowledgment of where the
intent behind it began.

**Short version:**

Annamacharya Kalabharati · Cuddapah · Regn. No. 217/1984. Sarasudha is a
new chapter inspired by that cultural journey.

## Post 4 — One Tradition. Many Expressions.

**Caption:**

Music doesn't stop at the boundaries we give it. Sarasudha holds Carnatic
depth, familiar light music, devotional feeling and contemporary
reinterpretation in the same conversation — because that's closer to how
people actually listen.

Carnatic. Light Music. Devotional. Contemporary. One tradition, many
expressions.

**Short version:**

Carnatic. Light Music. Devotional. Contemporary. One tradition, many

## Post 5 — Preserve. Perform. Pass On.

**Caption:**

Three words shape what we're building:

Preserve — document music, people, stories and cultural memory.
Perform — create real spaces where music is heard and reinterpreted.
Pass On — connect generations, and give younger artists room to begin.

A space for memory, music and the next generation.

**Short version:**

Preserve. Perform. Pass on. A space for memory, music and the next
generation.

## Post 6 — Be Part of What Comes Next

**Caption:**

Sarasudha doesn't have a roster of artists or a calendar of events yet —

If you perform, collaborate, hold family archives, or just want to follow

**Short version:**

Musicians, collaborators, archive contributors, listeners — there's a
