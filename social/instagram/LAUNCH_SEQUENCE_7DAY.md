# 7-day accelerated launch sequence

An alternative to `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md`'s 2-week plan, for a launch that
wants all six posts live within a single week rather than spread across
thirteen days. Use whichever cadence fits — both are honest, neither
invents content; the difference is only pacing. Day numbers are relative
to launch day.

| Day | Feed post | Story |
| --- | --------- | ----- |
| Day 1 | Profile goes live (name, bio, avatar, all 5 Highlights set with covers). Publish **Post 1 — Introducing Sarasudha**. | Repost Post 1 to Story. |
| Day 2 | No feed post — let Post 1 breathe. | "Coming Up" teaser previewing The Name, with a **question sticker**: "What does the name Sarasudha make you curious about?" |
| Day 3 | Publish **Post 2 — The Name**. | Repost Post 2 to Story. |
| Day 4 | Publish **Post 3 — Rooted in 1984**. | **Poll sticker**: "If Sarasudha hosted its first evening of music — would you be there?" (Yes / Maybe). Phrased as a hypothetical, not an announcement — this gauges real interest and gives an honest reason to eventually say "you told us yes, here's the first one," without claiming an event that isn't confirmed. |
| Day 5 | Publish **Post 4 — One Tradition. Many Expressions.** | Repost Post 4 to Story. |
| Day 6 | Publish **Post 5 — Preserve. Perform. Pass On.** | Participation call: "Are you a musician? We want to hear from you." → link sticker to `/participate`. |
| Day 7 | Publish **Post 6 — Be Part of What Comes Next**. | Recap Story: a quick montage of the week's six posts, capped with the participate link. |

## Why this pacing

- **Day 2 has no feed post on purpose** — publishing all six posts back to
  back reads as a content dump; a one-day pause with only Story activity
  keeps the account active without crowding the grid.
- **The Day 4 poll** is the one piece of content in this sequence that
  invites a real answer rather than just informing — worth watching the
  responses, since a strong "yes" is a legitimate signal for planning an
  actual first event later (see `docs/CONTENT_GUIDE.md` in the main repo:
  never publish an invented event, but real interest signals are fair game
  to act on).
- **Day 6's participation call** lands right before the final "be part of
  what comes next" post the next day, so the two reinforce each other
  instead of competing for attention on the same day.

## After Day 7

Same guidance as the 2-week plan: move to a slower, sustainable cadence
built from real developments (a new artist, a recovered archive item, a
confirmed event) rather than continuing to invent content.
